# Sample Library Licenses

This document tracks the licensing status of all audio samples included in Aetheria's factory library.

## Critical Requirements

**All factory samples must be**:
1. Originally created/recorded by Spaz SsG, OR
2. Purchased with explicit commercial redistribution rights, OR
3. Licensed under terms that permit commercial redistribution in software products

**Free download DOES NOT equal redistribution rights.**

## Verification Process

Before adding any sample to the factory library:

1. **Document the source**: Where was it obtained?
2. **Verify the license**: What are the exact terms?
3. **Confirm redistribution**: Does the license explicitly allow redistribution in commercial products?
4. **Save proof**: Keep license files, receipts, or agreements
5. **Add to this register**: Document below

## Recommended Sample Sources

### Original Recording
- **Best option**: Record your own samples
- **Pros**: Full ownership, unique character, no licensing concerns
- **Cons**: Requires equipment, instruments, and time

### Commercial Sample Libraries with Redistribution Rights

Research libraries that explicitly allow redistribution in software products. Examples of terms to look for:
- "Royalty-free for commercial use including redistribution"
- "May be embedded in software products"
- "Sample library license for software developers"

**Note**: Most standard sample libraries do NOT allow redistribution, even if royalty-free.

### Sample Library Developers

Contact sample library developers directly to negotiate redistribution licenses. Some may offer developer licenses for embedding in products.

### Public Domain

- Samples recorded before 1928 (in US)
- Samples explicitly released to public domain (CC0)
- **Caution**: Verify authenticity of public domain claims

---

## Prohibited Sources

**DO NOT use samples from**:
- "Free sample packs" without explicit redistribution rights
- Torrents or pirated content
- Samples ripped from other commercial products
- User-uploaded sample sites without verified licensing
- Creative Commons "Attribution" or "NonCommercial" licenses (unless you can comply with their terms)

---

## User-Imported Samples

Samples imported by Aetheria users via the user library feature are:
- **Not redistributed** with Aetheria
- **User's responsibility** for licensing
- **Not covered** by this document

User library is stored separately from factory library.
